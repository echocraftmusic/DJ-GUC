## Browser autoplay note

Modern browsers commonly block audible autoplay until the visitor interacts with the page. The player is designed to attempt normal playback only after the visitor presses Play. This protects the site from inconsistent or intrusive browser behavior while keeping stop, pause, switch, and seek controls available.
